Privacy Policy

Flow Observer for Freedom · Effective November 8, 2026

Flow Observer for Freedom ("the extension") is a free Chrome extension that shows charts of your own Freedom focus sessions. This policy explains what data it touches and where that data goes.

What the extension reads

When you are signed in to freedom.to in Chrome, the extension reads your Freedom session history from freedom.to: each session's name, start time, end time and duration. It uses your existing freedom.to login to do this. It never sees or stores your Freedom password.

Where it is stored

That session history, and your choice of Focus, Sleep or Ignore for each session name, is stored only in your browser using Chrome's local extension storage. It is not sent to the developer or to any other server, and it is not synced to other devices.

What the extension does not do

  • No analytics, tracking, advertising or telemetry.
  • No servers operated by the developer, and no account to create.
  • No access to any website other than freedom.to.
  • No selling, sharing or transferring of your data to anyone.

Deleting your data

Removing the extension from Chrome deletes everything it stored. Your history on freedom.to is not affected.

Relationship to Freedom

This is an independent project. It is not made by, affiliated with or endorsed by Freedom (80pct Solutions). Freedom's own handling of your data is covered by Freedom's privacy policy.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date. Questions: open an issue on the project's GitHub page.
